# RareEarth
A Rimworld mod that allows you to extract Rare Earth Minerals from rock chunks.

This is just a dump of the entire workshop folder, you can find the mod on the workshop here;

https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2189917314



---------

This mod adds the usage of Rare Earth Smelters into the game which allow you to extract rare earth minerals including Steel, Gold, Silver, Plasteel, Jade, and now Uranium from stone chunks. The larger the smelter, the more minerals it yields at a higher power cost and work time.

There are 4 models of the Rare Earth Smelters:
-Small Rare Earth Smelter
-Rare Earth Smelter
-Heavy Duty Rare Earth Smelter
-Giant Rare Earth Smelter
